# Privacy Policy — Xleuth

**Last updated:** October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Xleuth collects, uses, shares, and protects
information when you access or use our website, applications, and related
services (the “Service”). Xleuth is currently provided as a testnet product,
and test assets have no monetary value. By using the Service, you acknowledge
the practices described in this Policy.

## 1. Who we are

Xleuth operates the Service. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at
[privacy@xleuth.xyz](mailto:privacy@xleuth.xyz) or through the Support page.

## 2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your wallet address, an
optional display name or alias, and any messages you send to support. When you
use the Service, we also collect information about your activity, including
orders, positions, balances, transactions, faucet claims, and other
interactions with the Service, as well as technical information such as IP
address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and referral source.

We do not require your name, email address, password, or payment card details
to use the core Service. If you contact us, we receive whatever information you
choose to include.

## 3. Blockchain and public data

Transactions involving the Service are recorded on a public test blockchain.
Wallet addresses, balances, positions, orders, transaction hashes, and related
data may be publicly visible and permanent. We cannot edit or delete
information recorded on a blockchain. Please do not use a wallet address that
you need to keep private.

## 4. How we use information

We use information to:

- provide, operate, and maintain the Service;
- authenticate your wallet and maintain your session;
- process orders, positions, balances, and transactions;
- enforce faucet limits;
- detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security issues;
- comply with applicable law and enforce our terms;
- communicate with you about the Service; and
- understand and improve how the Service works.

## 5. Legal bases for processing

Where required by applicable law, we process personal information on the basis
of performing our contract with you, our legitimate interests in operating and
securing the Service, compliance with legal obligations, or your consent. You
may withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent, without
affecting processing that occurred before withdrawal.

## 6. How we share information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the
Service, such as hosting, wallet authentication, blockchain infrastructure,
market data, analytics, and customer support providers, subject to appropriate
confidentiality and data protection obligations. We may also share information
with professional advisers, authorities or other parties when required by law
or necessary to protect rights and safety, and with a successor or acquirer in
connection with a business transaction. We may share information with your
consent or at your direction. Information recorded on a public blockchain is
inherently public.

## 7. Cookies and similar technologies

We use strictly necessary cookies and local storage to keep you signed in,
maintain your wallet connection, remember interface preferences, and protect
the Service. If we enable analytics, it is limited to aggregated usage
information, is designed not to identify you, and does not use advertising
cookies. We honor Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control where supported. You
can control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling strictly
necessary storage may prevent parts of the Service from working.

## 8. Data retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the Service, meet
legal, security, and accounting requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce our
agreements. Because the Service operates in a testnet environment, some or all
app-side data may be reset or deleted without individual notice. Backup copies
may be retained for a limited period. Information recorded on a public
blockchain may be retained permanently by the network and cannot be deleted by
us.

## 9. How we protect information

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect
information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, and alteration. However,
no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot
guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting your wallet,
credentials, and devices.

## 10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete,
restrict, or object to the processing of your personal information, request
portability, withdraw consent, or lodge a complaint with a supervisory
authority. To exercise these rights, contact us at
[privacy@xleuth.xyz](mailto:privacy@xleuth.xyz).

We may need to verify that you control the relevant wallet before acting on a
request. Information recorded on a public blockchain cannot be changed or
deleted, and we may retain certain information where required by law.

## 11. International transfers

Information may be processed in countries other than the one in which you
live. Where required by applicable law, we use appropriate safeguards for such
transfers.

## 12. Children

The Service is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to
children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under
18. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will take
reasonable steps to delete it.

## 13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise
the “Last updated” date above. If a change is material, we will provide
additional notice where appropriate. Your continued use of the Service after an
update means you acknowledge the revised Policy.

## 14. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices,
contact us at [privacy@xleuth.xyz](mailto:privacy@xleuth.xyz) or through the
Support page.
